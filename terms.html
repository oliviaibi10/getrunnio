2. Description of the service

Runnio is an iOS app that lets runners:

  • Log and manage their running shoes
  • Track the kilometres accumulated by each shoe
  • Import workouts from Apple Health or Strava
  • View performance statistics
  • Get shoe rotation and replacement recommendations
  • Create and share images of their workouts

3. Plans and subscriptions

Runnio offers two plans:

  • Free plan: limited access to basic features (up to 2 shoes).
  • Runnio Pro: full access to all features.

Runnio Pro is available as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time purchase ("Runnio Lifetime") that unlocks Pro permanently, with no renewals. The exact price of each option is shown on the subscription screen inside the app, in your local currency.

Automatic renewal: Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. Runnio has no access to your payment details.
